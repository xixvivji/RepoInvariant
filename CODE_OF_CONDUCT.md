# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in RepoInvariant a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate in technical discussion and review.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior promptly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive concern, contact the maintainer through the repository's issue tracker. If a
report needs a private channel, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to arrange one and do not
include names, incident details, secrets, or private repository content in that public request.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
exclude participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
privately as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed where relevant.
